Our Story

Brandquist IT was founded on the belief that technology should be an enabler, not a barrier. With over 10+ years of IT experience, I saw how often small businesses and individuals struggled with technology issues that could easily be resolved with the right expertise and approach.

What started as helping friends and neighbors with computer problems has grown into a comprehensive IT service business that serves clients ranging from individual users to small and medium businesses. Our focus remains the same: providing personalized, reliable IT solutions that actually work for real people.

We take pride in building lasting relationships with our clients. Many of our customers have been with us for years, trusting us with everything from emergency computer repairs to strategic technology planning for business growth.
